# Color Foundation

## Role of color

Kingfisher color is derived from the bird, water, stone, and quiet landscape. Color should create orientation and emotional character without turning the interface into a rainbow dashboard.

## Semantic palette

### Deep Navy
Primary structural dark. Used for app shell, major dark surfaces, navigation, command surfaces, and dark mode.

### Petrol
Secondary structural dark. Used for layered panels, hover states, contextual surfaces, and graph depth.

### Kingfisher Blue
Primary brand/action color. Used for selected navigation, active graph nodes, important links, and controlled emphasis.

### Water Teal
Used for relationships, timeline lines, secondary informational emphasis, and calm interactive states.

### Copper / Orange
Attention and energy accent. Used sparingly for urgency, selected attention, bird plumage references, and important moments.

### Warm White
Primary light-mode canvas. Should feel natural rather than sterile.

### Stone
Neutral field for borders, subdued cards, dividers, secondary backgrounds, and metadata.

### Natural Green
Limited semantic support for completion, healthy status, and resolved states.

## Rules

- Never use more than one dominant accent color in a single information cluster.
- Copper/orange is never the default CTA color across the entire product.
- Destructive states require a distinct semantic red and must not reuse copper.
- Graph colors communicate entity/state meaning, not decoration.
- Light and dark mode preserve semantic roles.
- AI features do not receive neon gradients or rainbow branding.

## Token lock

Exact HEX/RGB values will be locked only after approval of the canonical Kingfisher master bird and first production screen, so the digital palette is sampled from the approved visual source rather than guessed independently.
