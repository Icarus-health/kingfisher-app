# Typography Foundation

Kingfisher uses two typographic voices.

## Editorial serif

Purpose: human-facing interpretation, reflection, greeting, briefing, major insight, and emotionally calm moments.

Use for:
- major page headings
- Morning Briefing opening
- important interpreted statements
- selected large numbers or time displays when the composition benefits

Do not use for:
- dense tables
- navigation
- long metadata lists
- compact controls

## Interface sans-serif

Purpose: precision, density, navigation, and operation.

Use for:
- navigation
- conversation UI
- task lists
- graph labels
- tables
- buttons
- filters
- metadata
- forms
- status labels

## Initial type scale

The final font families will be locked after Round-1 visual review. Until then the scale is authoritative and the family selection remains provisional.

- Display: 48 / 56, semibold
- H1: 32 / 38, medium–semibold
- H2: 24 / 30, medium
- H3: 18 / 24, medium
- Body: 16 / 24, regular
- UI: 14 / 20, regular–medium
- Label: 12 / 16, medium
- Meta: 11 / 16, regular

## Rules

- Dense does not mean tiny. Critical UI text must remain comfortably readable on a laptop.
- Serif and sans-serif should never compete at equal hierarchy in the same component.
- Avoid all-caps except for short structural labels.
- Avoid excessive tracking.
- Numerals used for time and dates should align cleanly and remain highly legible.
- Coding agents may not substitute arbitrary fonts when approved font families are available.
